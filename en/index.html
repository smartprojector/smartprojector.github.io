Features

Real numbers, not promises

Throw calculator

Distance, image size, zoom, lens shift and keystone, with the formulas explained and the same conventions manufacturers use.

Projector finder

Describe your room and lighting: the app rules out incompatible models and explains, criterion by criterion, why the others are ranked.

Try it in AR

Point your phone at the wall and see the real-scale image size, even comparing several models.

Verified catalog

Nearly 1,500 models from 57 brands, using only data from official manufacturer spec sheets. When a value is missing, the app says so.

How it works

From your room to the right model

Measure the room

Distance to the wall, size and lighting: a few values are enough, the rest is optional.

Compare models

A purely technical ranking, with trade-offs and missing data in plain sight.

Try it on the wall

Real-scale augmented-reality preview, then search for the model in stores.

Transparency

No paid rankings

The ranking depends only on the specs and your room. No manufacturer pays to appear or to rank higher.

The buttons to search for a model in stores may contain affiliate links: they are labeled and do not affect the ranking.

The app is free with an ad banner, shown only after your consent choice. A one-time purchase removes it.
