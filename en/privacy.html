Privacy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy covers the Smart Projector app (Android and iOS) and this website. It explains which data is processed, by whom and why. The Italian version is the reference text.

Controller and contact

Data controller: axelfolie, developer of the app. Contact: axelfolie@gmail.com.

In short

  • No account is needed, and the developer runs no servers that receive your personal data.
  • Calculations, settings, favorites and saved setups stay on your device.
  • The camera, used for the augmented-reality preview, is processed on the device only: images are not sent to anyone.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob. In the European Union the app asks for your consent before loading ads, and you can change it at any time.
  • In-app purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store: the developer never sees your payment details.

Ads and consent

The app shows a banner provided by Google AdMob. In the European Union, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google’s consent form appears before any ad is loaded. It is a consent management platform certified for the IAB Europe Transparency & Consent Framework. You can review or withdraw your choices at any time in Settings → Manage ad preferences. If you decline, non-personalized ads may appear; they still use some technical data, for example to limit fraud and frequency.

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy; see also how Google uses information from partner apps. If you buy the ad-free version you see no ads, and the app does not start the advertising system.

Store links and affiliate programs

On each model’s page you can search for the product on Amazon, eBay or Trovaprezzi. The links open external websites, which handle data under their own policies. Some links contain an affiliate code and are labeled “Affiliate link”: if you buy, the developer may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. The developer only receives aggregated sales reports from these programs, without your personal data.

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Legal bases

  • Consent: for personalized ads and for device operations that require it, such as the advertising ID.
  • Performance of a contract: for the in-app purchase, handled by the store.
  • Legitimate interest: to reply to emails and, for non-personalized ads, for security and fraud prevention by Google.

Transfers outside the European Union

Google and the stores may process data outside the EU, with the safeguards required by the GDPR (for example standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework).

Retention

Data on the device stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. Emails are kept as long as needed to reply and handle the request. Data processed by Google and the stores follows their retention periods.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability of your data, object to processing and withdraw consent at any time by writing to axelfolie@gmail.com. For data processed by Google or the stores you can also contact them. You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, or with the authority of your country.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their data.

This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics or third-party fonts or scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical visit data such as IP addresses for security (see GitHub’s privacy statement).

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated. Significant changes are also announced in the app.
